Meta AdsClient project · verified results

Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns for a footwear store

A local footwear retailer wanted more customers asking about products on WhatsApp, where their team could answer size and price questions and close the sale.

Business type
Footwear retailer, Pakistan
Period
5 Aug – 3 Sep 2026
Tools used
Meta Ads Manager, Meta Business Suite, WhatsApp

Challenge

Promote two different product lines — kids' school shoes and unisex lace-up sneakers — on a modest daily budget, and get people to start a conversation rather than just like a post.

Strategy

  • Use campaigns that send people straight into a WhatsApp chat, so interested buyers can ask questions immediately.
  • Run a separate campaign for each product line, so each audience and message could be judged on its own numbers.
  • Keep daily budgets around Rs 1,000 and track cost per conversation as the main success measure.

Execution

  • Campaign and ad set setup in Meta Ads Manager.
  • Product-focused ad copy for each line.
  • Daily monitoring of delivery, reach and cost per conversation.

Deliverables

Two live campaigns, ad copy, and a performance summary from the Ads Manager export.

SEOClient project · verified data

Local SEO for a German tiling company

A tile installation company in the Stuttgart region had a German-language website with a page for each city it serves — but very little search visibility.

Business type
Tile installation, Baden-Württemberg, Germany
Period
2026
Tools used
Google Search Console, keyword research tools

Challenge

The city landing pages shared almost identical text and images, several headings didn't match what people were searching for, and some technical issues were holding pages back.

Strategy

  • Research the German local queries customers really use, such as “fliesenleger stuttgart” and “fliesen waiblingen”.
  • Give each important page one clear primary keyword and align its H1 with it.
  • Make each city page genuinely local instead of a copy of the Stuttgart page.

Execution

  • Page-by-page audit of the homepage, service and city pages.
  • Flagged H1s not focused on the main keyword, a call-to-action styled as large as the H1, and repeated headings on the bathroom renovation page.
  • Found that every city page reused the Stuttgart image alt text, and recommended unique alt text and a unique local section per city.
  • Found cart and checkout links pointing to a test domain and recommended replacing them with the live domain.
  • On-page and content optimization, then tracked queries in Search Console in 28-day comparisons.

Deliverables

Keyword research, an issues-and-fixes sheet for each audited URL, on-page optimization and Search Console reporting.

Keyword research for a self-service car wash

A self-service car wash park in Pforzheim, Germany needed to know how local drivers search, so its website could target the right terms.

Business type
Car wash park, Pforzheim, Germany
Market
Germany, German-language search
Tools used
Keyword research tools (volume & difficulty data)

Challenge

Car wash searches are highly local and often happen on a phone, on the way. The business needed a clear list of terms worth targeting, with an English translation for each.

Strategy

  • Prioritise “near me” and city-specific searches, where a local business can realistically compete.
  • Favour keywords with low difficulty scores for faster wins.
  • Group terms into themes that can each become a page or section: self-service wash, “nearby / open now”, Pforzheim local, interior cleaning and vacuum, engine wash.

Execution

Researched and documented 23 German keywords with monthly search volume, keyword difficulty and an English meaning.

Google AdsStrategy blueprint

Search Ads plan for a local dental clinic

A complete Google Search campaign plan for a local dental clinic — from keyword research and campaign structure to ad copy and conversion tracking.

Business type
Local dental clinic
Goal
Appointment bookings and calls
Tools
Google Ads, Google Keyword Planner

Challenge

The clinic wants more weekend bookings from people nearby, without wasting budget on job seekers, students or people looking for free advice.

Strategy

  • One Search campaign targeting a set radius around the clinic, scheduled around opening hours.
  • Ad groups split by intent: checkups & cleaning, emergency dentist, teeth whitening.
  • Phrase and exact match keywords, plus a shared negative list (jobs, course, salary, free, DIY).

Execution

  • Responsive search ads with headlines that match each ad group's search intent.
  • Call, location and sitelink assets.
  • Conversion tracking plan for online bookings and calls.
  • Weekly search terms review to add negatives and new keywords.

Deliverables

Campaign structure, keyword and negative keyword lists, ad copy, and a tracking checklist.

How success is measured

Success is measured by cost per booking, conversion rate and the quality of the search terms triggering ads, reviewed weekly after launch.

Email MarketingStrategy blueprint

Email automation for a skincare e-commerce store

An email automation plan for an online skincare store, built to win first orders and bring customers back for repeat purchases.

Business type
E-commerce skincare brand
Goal
More first orders and repeat purchases
Platform
Any standard email platform, e.g. Mailchimp

Challenge

The store pays for ads to win each customer, but most buyers never order again, and many shoppers leave items in their cart.

Strategy

  • Welcome sequence (3 emails) to introduce the brand and help new subscribers choose a routine.
  • Abandoned-cart sequence (2 emails, after 1 hour and 24 hours) that stops as soon as someone buys.
  • Post-purchase sequence (3 emails) with usage tips, a review request and a refill reminder.
  • Segments: new subscribers, first-time buyers, repeat buyers and customers inactive for 90 days.

Deliverables

Flow map, eight email drafts, segmentation plan and a monthly reporting template.

How success is measured

Success is measured by click rate, revenue from each flow and repeat purchase rate, reviewed monthly.
